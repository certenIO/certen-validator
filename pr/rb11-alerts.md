# RB11 staged finality: alert thresholds

All series are exported on the validator's `/metrics`; the same state (without history) is the `finality` member of
`/health/detailed`. None of these alerts concerns settlement, attestation or write-back: the finality upgrader and the capture
loops sit beside them. A level a leg lacks is a named state, never a failure of the leg. Thresholds are starting values for the
first week in shadow; tune them from the measured per-chain etas of V2_SPEC_S7 8.1.

| Metric | Condition | Severity | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `certen_finality_upgrader_enabled` | `== 0` on any validator (or differs between validators) | warning, page if the fleet was meant to be on | The default is ON; an off switch is the named state `finality_upgrader_disabled` (log line and `certen_finality_upgrader_disabled_total{chain="0"}`). A validator that stops appending leaves its legs at `included` for as long as no other validator appends. |
| `certen_finality_chain_enabled{chain}` | `== 0` for a chain that has live legs | warning | A per-chain switch (`CERTEN_FINALITY_CHAIN_<id>_ENABLED=false`) or missing provider configuration; the reason is in `/health/detailed` (`disabled_reason`) and in `certen_finality_upgrader_disabled_total{chain,env}`. |
| `increase(certen_finality_upgrader_disabled_total[1h])` | `> 0` after a deploy that was not meant to switch anything off | warning | One increment per announced named state: the setting that caused it is the `env` label. |
| `increase(certen_finality_build_held_total{code="l1_capture_gap"}[6h])` | `> 0` for more than 24 hours after a deploy | ticket | Legs inside a recorded capture gap are HELD, not failed (RB12-GO-INT F17): each is asked again every `PendingRetry` (2 minutes, bounded poll) with no failure back-off, so the counter rises by one per held level per ask and the leg is built within one retry interval of the backfill closing the hole. Rising for a day means the hole is not closing (no archive endpoint, `CERTEN_FINALITY_ARCHIVE_URLS`): run `pr/rb12-capture-gaps.sh`. Held asks are not in `certen_finality_build_failures_total`. |
| `certen_finality_capture_lag_blocks{chain,capturer}` | `> 60` for 10 minutes | warning; `> 90` for 5 minutes: critical | Tenderly serves `eth_getProof` only about 8 blocks below the head and the other public providers at most 100. A capture loop further behind than the window records `l1_proof_window_unavailable` / `batcher_state_unavailable` and the missed block can then only be redone from an archive or paid provider (`CERTEN_FINALITY_ARCHIVE_URLS`). |
| `time() - certen_finality_last_append_unixtime{chain}` | `> 2 x` the slowest level's eta of the chain while `certen_finality_legs{level!="finalized"}` is non-zero | warning | An upgrader that is on but appends nothing. Etas: Sepolia and Base `l1_posted` about 20 min, Arbitrum `finalized` about 110 min, Base `finalized` about 5.3 days, Adiri up to 6 h + 5 min. Do not alert for Base `finalized` on this metric alone. |
| `time() - certen_finality_last_pass_unixtime` | `> 5 x` the pass interval (default 60 s; use 10 min) | critical | The upgrader's pass loop stopped advancing (a hung store is bounded by `StoreTimeout`, so this means the process lost the loop). |
| `increase(certen_finality_capturer_restarts_total{chain,capturer}[1h])` | `> 5` | warning | A capture loop that keeps failing or panicking; the supervisor restarts it with a bounded backoff, the failing state is in the log. |
| `increase(certen_finality_status_total{state=~"l1_proof_window_unavailable\|batcher_state_unavailable\|l1_blob_unavailable\|finality_lc_unavailable"}[30m])` | `> 0` and rising for 30 minutes | warning | The builder could not establish an input it needs (a missed provider window, a beacon blob API without the blobs, no light-client endpoint). Each is a named state in `proof_finality_status`; the leg keeps the level it has. OD3 (own beacon node) and OD4 (archive provider) remove the usual causes. |
| `rate(certen_finality_provider_throttled_total{provider_host}[1h])` | `> 30` per hour for one host | warning | HTTP 429 backoffs. The limiter slows down by itself; a sustained rate means the per-provider rate limit is above what the provider allows (the Arbitrum public RPC answers 429 to batches of 100 requests; batches are capped at 40). The label is a host name only. |
| `increase(certen_finality_capture_refused_total{chain,code}[1h])` | `> 0` | warning | A capture refused by a named state, for example `finality_evidence_too_large` when a block's rows exceed `CERTEN_FINALITY_MAX_CAPTURE_BYTES_PER_LEG`. Nothing is truncated; the block is not captured and the state names why. |
| `rate(certen_finality_capture_bytes_total{chain}[1h])` | above 4 MiB per captured leg on average, or growing faster than the chain's observed traffic | info | The capture tables are insert-only and nothing is deleted: this is the disk growth rate to budget (Base about 1.1 MB per leg with blobs). |
| `increase(certen_finality_chain_broken_total{chain}[5m])` | `> 0` | page | A stored addendum chain failed verification against the leg binding recomputed from the verified Settlement. Should never be non-zero; nothing is appended to that leg until it is looked at. |
| `increase(certen_finality_build_failures_total{code="finality_verifier_failed"}[1h])` | `> 0` | warning | A builder produced evidence its own registered Kind refuses: a bug or a provider answer that parses but does not verify; nothing was stored. |
| `increase(certen_finality_status_write_failures_total{chain}[15m])` | `> 3` | info | The status table is not authoritative; the next pass writes it again. A burst means the database is slow, which also bounds the upgrader. |
| `certen_finality_spine_stalled_attempts_max` | `>= 3` for 30 min on any validator (the Sepolia spine of a leg gained no headers in hand for three attempts in a row, whoever fetched them; attempts are about 5 to 9 min apart; a leg not attempted for an hour is forgotten) | warning | RB12-SEPOLIA-SPINE. A leg in `finality_pending{awaiting_spine}` is normal while `have` grows; this is the same state without growth: a provider that throttles or fails, or a store that refuses writes. `certen_finality_spine_legs_awaiting` says how many legs are waiting for their spine, `increase(certen_finality_spine_headers_total{outcome="fetched"}[1h])` how much was fetched, `certen_finality_spine_attempts_total{outcome}` how attempts end (budget, throttled, error) and `certen_finality_spine_rows_invalid_total{reason}` counts kept rows that were not what their key says (replaced from the provider in the process; the table cannot be corrected). |
| `certen_finality_prefix_windows{chain="84532",state="unavailable_proven"}` or `{state="retention_expired"}` | `> 0` for 1 hour | ticket (owner: OD4) | RB12-ARCHIVE-BACKFILL: a leg posted before the capture started has a block whose blobs two or more beacon hosts proved gone (or past retention). Its state is `l1_capture_gap{gap_state=unavailable_proven, needs=blob_archive}` with the newest gone slot and the oldest slot that was served; only a blob archive (the owner's OD4 key or an own beacon node) closes it. The backfill asks again once a day (the newest block of each run, expired or proven) and stops asking the rest of the run while that block is still gone. |
| `certen_finality_prefix_windows{chain=~"84532|421614",state="open"}` | `> 0` for 24 hours with `rate(certen_finality_prefix_backfilled_blocks_total[1h]) == 0` | warning | The prefix backfill is not advancing: no validator takes the chunks (see `certen_finality_prefix_lease_total{outcome="error"}`), the archive provider is unset (`CERTEN_FINALITY_ARCHIVE_URLS`) so no old block can be proven, or the memory budget refuses every tick. Window gauges are set on every backfill tick, from the first, so an absent series means the loop is not running. |
| `increase(certen_finality_prefix_lease_total{outcome="error"}[1h])` | `> 0` | warning | The store could not take a claim on a chunk of prefix blocks: that validator fetched no prefix block that tick (no claim, no fetch). `outcome="lost"` is normal: another validator holds the chunk. |
| `certen_finality_index_lag_blocks{chain,family}` | `> 100` for 30 min, not counting the first hour after a deploy (the first backfill of the capture index has a lag by construction) | warning | RB12 capture index. The index builds from the captured blocks; behind the capture, a leg whose window is not yet indexed stays `finality_pending{l1_posted_unproven}` judged `index_behind` (it is asked again, never failed). `family="bi"` is Base, `family="arb"` Arbitrum. A lag that does not shrink: read `certen_finality_index_step_seconds` and the log of the index loop. |
| `certen_finality_index_disabled` | `== 1` on any validator | warning | A bad index setting switched the index off by the named state `finality_index_disabled{chain, variable, reason}` (the `variable` label is the setting; the value is never shown). The window reader serves; finality is otherwise unchanged. |
| `certen_finality_prefix_disabled` | `== 1` on any validator | warning | A bad prefix (archive backfill) setting switched the prefix feature of one chain off by the named state `finality_prefix_disabled{chain, variable, reason, refused}` (`key` label = the setting). The upgrader, the capture loops, the gap backfill of the live range and the index are unchanged; a leg that needs a prefix window says `l1_capture_not_covering{prefix_state: off}`. |
| `increase(certen_finality_index_notes_dropped_total{chain,family}[1h])` | `> 0` | info | More late-block notes than the pending set holds. Base drops the note (the rotating walk reaches the block later); Arbitrum folds notes into ranges and still works them off. Nothing is lost; a steady rate means a backfill filled far more blocks than the index could be told of at once. |
| `increase(certen_finality_index_entries_skipped_total{chain,family,reason}[1h])` | `> 0` | info | A complete channel that gets no index entry (`reason="over_window"`: its frames lie further apart than the sequencing window, so no leg can be served by it). The reference reader is unchanged. |
| `certen_finality_index_wide_pointers{chain,family}` | `> 0` for 1 hour | warning | Arbitrum batches whose time bounds are wider than the normal lookup window: expected 0 in production. Above the pointer limit a lookup names `index_inconsistent{coverage_overflow}` rather than answer from a partial listing. |
| `increase(certen_finality_index_mismatches_total[1h])` or `increase(certen_finality_index_conflicts_total[1h])` | `> 0` | page | The index disagrees with the rows it was computed from (`index_inconsistent{self_check_mismatch}`), or a write met a row with different bytes. Run `validator finality-index-selfcheck --sample 4` (and `finality-arb-index-selfcheck`) on that validator. Rows are insert-only; a new `CERTEN_FINALITY_*_INDEX_GENERATION` rebuilds. |
| `increase(certen_finality_index_unindexable_blocks_total{chain,family,reason}[1h])` | `> 0` | warning | A captured block the indexer could not read; a leg whose window holds it and whose channel is missing says `index_block_unindexable{reason}`. The rows of the block are stored and do not change: this needs a look, not a retry. |
| `increase(certen_finality_capture_batcher_unestablished_total{chain}[1h])` | `> 0` | warning | Captured blocks whose rows cannot establish the SystemConfig batcher: their frames are kept as candidates and the verifier names the fault. |
| `increase(certen_finality_capture_foreign_txs_total{chain}[1h])` | a step change from the validator's own baseline | info | Batch-inbox transactions sent by a key that is not the batcher of their block are skipped by the capture and by every reader (one authorization rule). Some are normal; a sudden rise is somebody writing to the inbox. |
| `certen_finality_legacy_scan_failing{chain}` | `== 1` for 15 min | warning | The last scan of the legacy-leg list failed: the counts of `certen_finality_legacy_headers` are then the legs known before it and zeros mean nothing. |
| `certen_finality_legacy_unwired{chain}` | `== 1` | warning | The legacy-header reconstruction loop could not be registered at startup (the validator runs without it; its legacy legs stay `finality_included_unavailable{no_block_header}`). |
| `certen_finality_legacy_headers{state="invalid"}` | `> 0` | ticket | A legacy leg whose stored component contradicts its own block header, or cannot be read: not repaired automatically. `state="unserved"` for 24 hours is the same ticket (no provider serves the block). |
| `increase(certen_finality_spine_rows_invalid_total[1h])` | `> 0` | warning | Kept Sepolia spine rows that were not what their key says (`finality_spine_row_invalid`); they are replaced from the provider, and the cause (a bad write or a changed provider answer) needs a look. |
